## 2026

### Dr. Marina López-Solà receives the Serra Húnter Talent Award 2026
**June 2026**

We are delighted to announce that our PI, **Dr. Marina López-Solà**, has been recognized with the [**Serra Húnter Talent Award 2026**](https://recercaiuniversitats.gencat.cat/ca/serra-hunter/comunitat/reconeixements/) in the *Research Excellence* (*Recerca d'Excel·lència*) category. The award, promoted by the Catalan Department of Research and Universities, was presented at a ceremony held at the Jardins del Palau Robert in Barcelona. Congratulations, Marina!
